## 2. Plain Narration Text (for Voice Generation)
```
Stealing a plane in New York should get you twenty years in prison. This guy did it twice, and only paid a hundred bucks. In nineteen fifty-six, drunk pilot Tommy Fitzpatrick slammed his beer mug down at O'Malley's Bar. He made a crazy bet he could fly from New Jersey to Manhattan in fifteen minutes flat. Nobody at the crowded table believed a word of his wild boast. So at three in the morning, Tommy crept across a moonlit New Jersey airfield. He broke inside a tiny propeller plane and blasted off into the night with zero lights. Tommy raced blind between glowing Manhattan skyscrapers straight toward Washington Heights. He plunged straight down a narrow New York avenue lined with parked vintage cars. Rubber tires screeched across dark asphalt right outside O'Malley's front door. Tommy casually strolled back inside grinning while his friend's jaw hit the floor. Cops surrounded the plane fast, yet the shocked owner refused to press charges. So a courtroom judge slammed his gavel and handed Tommy a hundred-dollar fine. Headlines made him famous, until two years later a new patron called him a liar. So Tommy walked outside and parked a second plane at the exact same bar twice!
```
