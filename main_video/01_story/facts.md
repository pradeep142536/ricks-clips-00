# The Army That Lost a War to Birds

The Great Emu War was a humorous name for a 1932 Australian pest-control operation in Western Australia. Drought pushed large emu mobs toward wheat farms, where they damaged crops and fences. Major G. P. W. Meredith led a small military party with Lewis machine guns. The operation struggled because the birds were fast, spread out, and difficult to target. A gun jammed during an ambush near a watering point. The military effort was withdrawn, and farmers ultimately relied more on fencing and barriers.

The narration uses “war” as a comic title but clearly explains that this was not a formal declared war.
